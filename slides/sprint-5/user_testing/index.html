User testing session

A short test of search and filter tasks in an interactive prototype.

GlobalData × Shoreditch · 5 October 2026

Context

GlobalData Healthcare and Shoreditch Design are testing a new way to search the Drugs database. A search can start as a plain sentence.

This test has seven tasks and one final question. Each task takes a few minutes.

Three user goals

From 176,404 existing advanced searches across the Drugs and Clinical Trials databases, we narrowed the work down to three user goals.

G1
Quick search
53% of searches use a single filter and 85% use three or fewer. A usable list has to be reachable in very few steps.
G2
Refined search
80% of follow-up searches keep the same fields and change a value. Only 12% add a filter. Adjusting a known query has to be fast and reversible.
G3
Power search
15% of searches use four or more filters, with up to 22 in one search. OR within a field appears in 19%, NOT in under 1%.
Analysts reach a usable list of drugs with less effort when they can describe the search in their own words, refine it in place, and switch to building it by hand without having to look for the option.
Hypothesis

Questions

01
Find all Phase III drugs in Europe that act on Janus Kinase. (G1)
02
Change the last search. Replace Europe with the United States. (G2)
03
Use plain language to find marketed, oral, small molecule drugs in the United States with a receptor-based mechanism of action. (G3)
04
Find the same drugs again. Choose each filter by hand. Do not type a sentence. (G3)

Questions, continued

05
Remove the route of administration from the search. (G2)
06
Show only the drugs from one company in the list. (G2)
07
Find Phase I gene therapies for dermatology in Brazil. If there are no results, change the search until results show.
08
How likely are you to recommend this way of searching to a colleague?

Instructions

01
One link per tester
Use the prototype link that you received. Do not share it.
02
What is being tested
The test checks how easy it is to find a list of drugs. It tests the design, not you.
03
How tasks are scored
After each task, give a score from 1 (Very difficult) to 5 (Very easy). Then give the reason. The final question uses a scale from 0 to 10.
04
This is a prototype
The data is a sample. Some parts do not work. If something does not respond, tell us what you expected.
